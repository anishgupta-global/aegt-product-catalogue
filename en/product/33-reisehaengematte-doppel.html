Double camping hammock (parachute nylon, tree straps and carabiners, stuff sack)

Ultralight double hammock made of parachute nylon with tree straps, carabiners and stuff sack: hung in seconds, packs small.

The double hammock made of breathable, quick-drying parachute nylon (e.g. 210T) carries 150 to 200 kg depending on the version and packs to the size of a fist. Tree straps and carabiners allow setup between two trees without knots; a mosquito net, tarp or sleeping mat can be added.
