Hazardous material remediation

Disposable asbestos day set (3x coverall type 5/6, 3x FFP3, 3x nitrile gloves, 3x overshoes, asbestos bag, cloth tape, warning sticker)

Disposable complete set for one working day in an asbestos environment (up to three entries): coverall, FFP3, gloves, overshoes, asbestos bag, tape and warning sticker in one carton.

All components are category III PPE and must each hold an EU type examination. The set replaces neither the risk assessment nor competence under TRGS 519; depending on the procedure a full-face mask or powered respirator may be needed. It suits short jobs with low fibre release (maintenance, repair), not large-scale remediation.
