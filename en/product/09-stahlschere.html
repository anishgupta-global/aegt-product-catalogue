Hydraulic steel shear (scrap shear) for excavators of the 18–27 t class, 360° rotating

Robust steel and scrap shear for dismantling, scrap yards and steel construction, cuts round steel (rating 30 mm), sections and plate.

The steel shear is the standard tool for dismantling steel structures, beams, pipes and scrap with the excavator, used in demolition, scrap recycling and industrial dismantling. Professionals look for blade material and geometry with reversible blades, a durable slewing unit, speed valve, case-drain connection and a declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive.
