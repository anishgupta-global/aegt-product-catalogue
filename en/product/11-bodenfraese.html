Concrete scarifier 250 mm, electric 400 V

Electric scarifier with a milling drum for removing coatings, adhesives, bitumen and loose screed layers and for roughening concrete.

The electric scarifier removes coatings, adhesives, bitumen residues, markings and loose screed layers with a rotating milling drum and roughens concrete for subsequent coatings. With a 250 mm working width and 7.5 kW drive it is the economical solution in demolition and renovation when hand tools would be too slow. Professional users expect a robust drum bearing, a quick cutter-ring change, a continuously adjustable milling depth and a large dust port for class H industrial vacuums. Prerequisites are CE marking under the Machinery Directive, electrics for 400 V three-phase and a German manual; where asbestos is suspected, use is possible only in a sealed-off area under TRGS 519.
