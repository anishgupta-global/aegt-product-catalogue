Aluminium lean-to ladder 12 rungs (EN 131), length about 3.4 m

Light single-section aluminium rung ladder for painting, facade and maintenance work, 150 kg load.

The lean-to ladder is the light single-section ladder leaned against a wall, roof edge or pole, covering painting, plastering, roofing and maintenance work. Buyers expect extruded aluminium stiles, non-slip rungs at 28 cm spacing, non-slip feet, a wall-friendly ladder top and an EN 131 test report stating the 150 kg maximum load.
