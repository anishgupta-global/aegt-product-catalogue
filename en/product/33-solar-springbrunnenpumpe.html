Solar fountain pump / solar pond pump (kit with solar panel and nozzle heads, approx. 180 l/h)

Solar-direct kit without battery: panel and brushless submersible pump for bird baths, ponds or ornamental fountains.

The kit consists of a solar panel (9 V / 4 W) and a brushless DC submersible pump (4.5–10 V) that runs directly on sunlight: no battery, no mains connection, no cabling to a socket. The pump suits bird baths, small ponds, water basins and ornamental fountains. High-quality kits stand out through a weatherproof panel with UV-resistant laminate, a fully potted pump, an intake filter against algae and an honest performance figure at defined irradiance.
