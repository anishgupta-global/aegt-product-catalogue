Respiratory protection

Storage box for half-mask respirators (hard-shell EVA case with zipper)

Moulded EVA case with water-repellent nylon cover, terry lining and foam insert for a reusable half mask and its filters.

The hard-shell EVA case holds a reusable half mask together with its filters and keeps it clean, dust-free and protected from knocks between uses. The heat-pressed EVA shell with water-repellent nylon cover, terry lining and die-cut foam insert protects the sealing lip and valves from deformation and dirt; the nylon zipper and webbing handle make it a robust companion in a tool case or van. Important for classification: the case is not a respiratory protection product and carries no PPE CE mark; because the zipper does not close airtight, it is no substitute for a sealed box for long-term storage of gas filters.
