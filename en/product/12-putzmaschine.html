Plastering machine (mortar spraying machine), electric 230/400 V

Conveying and spraying machine for machine-applied plaster, gypsum and lime-cement plaster, indoors and outdoors.

The plastering machine mixes dry mortar with water and conveys it through a hose to the spray gun; on large refurbishment and finishing areas it replaces manual application and saves considerable time with gypsum, lime and lime-cement plaster. The machine needs CE marking under the Machinery Directive, electrical equipment to EN 60204-1, a German operating manual and an inspection under DGUV Vorschrift 3. Only plaster mortars marked for machine application may be processed.
