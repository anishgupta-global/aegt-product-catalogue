Construction robotics

Wall tile paving robot (interior walls, thin-bed)

Bonds wall tiles in thin-bed adhesive on prepared interior walls and relieves tilers of strenuous wall work.

Professional users in Germany expect execution to DIN 18352 with tile adhesive to DIN EN 12004-1, a substrate to DIN 18202, CE conformity under the Machinery Directive and a German manual.

Technical data

Tile size300 × (450–600) mm
Laying methodThin-bed adhesive bonding on the wall
Substrateflat, load-bearing interior wall to DIN 18202, primed
DocumentationCE declaration of conformity, risk assessment, German manual
Customs tariff number (HS)84795000
