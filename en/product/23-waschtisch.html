Ceramic washbasin 60 cm, wall-hung

White wall-hung washbasin in sanitary porcelain with tap hole and overflow for staff and sanitary rooms.

The wall-hung washbasin in glazed sanitary porcelain permanently equips staff rooms, sanitary rooms and refurbished bathrooms with a hygienic washing facility. The smooth, low-porosity glaze is easy to clean and resists common cleaning and disinfecting agents. A professional user in Germany expects accurate connecting dimensions per EN 31, a load-tested body per EN 14688 and fixing material for safe wall mounting.
