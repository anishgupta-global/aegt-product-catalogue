5-in-1 multi air styler (attachments for curls, smoothing, volume)

Dryer base unit with auto-wrap curler, paddle brush, oval brush and concentrator for drying and styling in one step.

The multi air styler combines a high-speed base unit with four attachments: auto-wrap curler, paddle brush, oval brush and concentrator. Warm air instead of heated plates is gentler on hair and attachments are swapped via a plug-in connection.
