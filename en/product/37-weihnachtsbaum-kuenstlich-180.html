Artificial Christmas tree 180 cm (PE/PVC mix, metal stand, flame-retardant)

Dense 180 cm Christmas tree made of a PE/PVC mix with a sturdy stand, compactly packed and reusable for years.

The 180 cm tree is made of a PE/PVC material mix that reproduces the growth of a fir with dense, evenly inserted branches. A sturdy metal stand provides safe footing; the tree ships compactly in a single carton and stores flat after the season. For EU distribution, GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person and REACH and POP compliance of the PVC parts are standard.
