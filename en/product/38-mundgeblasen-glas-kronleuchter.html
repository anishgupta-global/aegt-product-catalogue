Mouth-blown glass chandelier

Handcrafted glass chandelier with mouth-blown shades and LED light source, a one-of-a-kind look for entrance halls, stairwells and dining rooms.

Each shade is blown by hand, so wall thickness, air inclusions and colour gradient vary slightly, which is the appeal of high-end glass lighting.

Light sourceLED, lifespan 30,000 h
