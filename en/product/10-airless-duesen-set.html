Construction robotics

Airless spray tip set, reversible (6 sizes, e.g. 211-523) with filter set

Six reversible tungsten carbide airless tips for common tip guards plus filters, for wall and ceiling coatings.

The orifice sets flow rate and fan width; carbide inserts withstand the abrasion of dispersion paints longer than simple steel tips, and the reversing function lets clogged tips be cleared without removal. Professional users expect clean orifice geometry, a dimensionally accurate fit in the tip guard and a fan free of tails.

Technical data

Size code1st digit x 2 = fan width in inches at 30 cm distance, last two digits = orifice in 1/1000 inch (517 = about 25 cm fan, 0.017 in = 0.43 mm)
