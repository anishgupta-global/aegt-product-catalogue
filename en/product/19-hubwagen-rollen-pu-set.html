Replacement load rollers for hand pallet trucks, polyurethane, 80 x 70 mm

PU load roller 80 x 70 mm for hand pallet trucks: quieter, floor-friendly replacement for worn fork rollers, can be assembled as a set of four.

The load roller (fork roller) carries the rated load of the hand pallet truck at the fork tip and is the most common wear part: polyurethane tread, size 80 x 70 mm, bearing seat 47 x 14 mm (corresponds to the 6204 deep-groove ball bearing with 20 mm bore). Quality markers are a firmly bonded tread that does not separate from the core, a precisely made bearing seat and dimensional accuracy in diameter and width, because neighbouring sizes such as 82 x 80 or 85 x 80 do not fit. Commercial buyers expect a load rating per roller, REACH compliance and a dimensional drawing; most trucks use four rollers (tandem) or two rollers, the steering wheels (180 or 200 x 50 mm) are a separate part.
