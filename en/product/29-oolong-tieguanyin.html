Oolong tea Tieguanyin (Anxi, Fujian), loose leaf, bag 100 g

Semi-fermented oolong with an orchid aroma, the premium step above loose jasmine green tea, packed in 100 g bags.

Tieguanyin is a semi-fermented oolong tea whose leaves are rolled into tight pellets that unfurl in hot water and give a pale yellow infusion with floral and fruity notes and a sweet aftertaste. Discerning buyers expect uniformly round, heavy pellets with little breakage and dust, a clear infusion without foreign flavours and demonstrably low pesticide residues.
