LED face mask (red and near-infrared light, silicone, USB battery)

Flexible silicone mask with 240 LEDs in four wavelengths for home use, USB rechargeable.

Four wavelengths (460, 580, 630 and 850 nm) cover blue, yellow, red and near-infrared light. The flexible design comes in sizes S, M and L and charges via USB (5 V/1 A). Buyers expect even illumination, a timer, eye protection and reliable safety documentation.
