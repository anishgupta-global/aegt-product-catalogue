Personal protective equipment (PPE)

Industrial climbing helmet EN 12492 (ABS, chin strap, dial adjustment)

Chin-strap helmet for work at height, rope access and rescue – protects against impact and lateral blows.

The industrial climbing helmet protects against impact and blows during rope access, work on façades, masts and scaffolding and in rescue operations. Unlike a standard hard hat it stays securely on the head with a chin strap and stepless dial adjustment even in a fall, when swinging or on lateral impact; the ABS shell is light and ventilated. Professional users in Germany expect certification to EN 12492, on construction sites additionally EN 397, PPE Category II with EU type-examination, a print of manufacturing date and standard edition and an accessory system for headlamp and face protection. For work with a risk of falling, the DGUV rules on personal fall-protection equipment apply in addition.
