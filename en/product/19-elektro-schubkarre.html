Electric power barrow (battery wheelbarrow) 300 kg

Battery-powered power barrow that moves heavy loads up ramps and over uneven ground without physical effort.

The electric power barrow replaces the classic wheelbarrow on demolition and strip-out sites wherever rubble, screed and material must be moved over long distances or ramps, and it relieves the backs and joints of the workforce. A professional unit has a powerful geared electric motor, a low-maintenance lithium battery with battery management, a steel or tipping tray as well as an emergency stop and a direction switch with dead-man function. Users in Germany expect CE marking under the Machinery Directive, EMC compliance, a German manual and battery and spare-parts service. Battery and charger are additionally subject to UN 38.3 and the EU Battery Regulation.
