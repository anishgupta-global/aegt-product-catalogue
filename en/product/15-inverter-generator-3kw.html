Petrol inverter generator 3 kW (silent)

Quiet inverter generator with 230 V sine-wave output for small sites and hand tools.

The silent 3 kW petrol inverter generator supplies small sites, container offices and hand tools with clean 230 V sine-wave power where no site distribution board is available. Professional users in Germany expect a compact, robust housing, low noise, low-oil shutdown, Schuko sockets with RCD protection and a tested voltage quality for sensitive equipment. CE under the Machinery Directive, the Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC with LwA marking, EMC and an engine to EU Stage V are mandatory; the GS mark is a strong quality argument in commercial use.
