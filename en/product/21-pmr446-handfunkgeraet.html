PMR446 handheld two-way radio (licence-free, site radio)

Licence-free analogue handheld radio for communication between site management, crews and machine operators on site.

The PMR446 handheld radio is the standard tool for communication between site management, demolition crews and machine operators when mobile coverage fails in buildings or basements. In Germany PMR446 may be used without registration and without fees, provided the device has a fixed antenna and transmits at no more than 0.5 W ERP.
