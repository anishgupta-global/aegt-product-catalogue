Steel locker / wardrobe cabinet, lockable

Lockable steel locker with ventilated doors for separate storage of work and private clothing in changing and break cabins.

The steel locker provides separate, lockable storage of employees' work and private clothing in changing and break cabins. Expect a torsion-resistant body of powder-coated sheet steel, deburred edges, ventilated doors and a cylinder or coin-deposit lock. Professional buyers in Germany require compliance with the requirements for changing rooms and wardrobes under ASR A4.1, stability tested to EN 16121/16122 and a spare-key service. Supplied knocked down, the locker saves freight; supplied welded, it saves assembly time on site.
