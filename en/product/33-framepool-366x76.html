Round frame pool set Ø 366 x 76 cm (steel frame, PVC composite liner, cartridge filter)

Round above-ground pool set (12 ft x 30 in) with steel-tube frame, multi-layer PVC liner and cartridge filter pump, assembled without tools.

The frame pool set with Ø 366 cm and a 76 cm wall height holds about 8 m3 to the brim by calculation and is the classic entry size for families in the garden. It consists of a powder-coated steel-tube frame that is plugged together without tools, a multi-layer PVC composite liner (triple layer with fabric insert) and a cartridge filter pump; depending on the set, a cover and ladder are included. Assembly takes a few hours, and in winter the pool can be dismantled and stored. Discerning buyers judge quality by tube wall thickness and corrosion protection, welded liner seams, a CE-compliant pump with EU plug and clearly visible warnings to EN 16582.
