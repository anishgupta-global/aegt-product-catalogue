Mobile whiteboard 180 × 90 cm, double-sided, magnetic

Rolling double-sided magnetic whiteboard 180 × 90 cm with aluminium frame, pen tray and castors for meetings and workshops.

The mobile whiteboard 180 × 90 cm turns every meeting room and project area into a workspace: writable on both sides, magnetic and freely movable on castors. Professional buyers expect a scratch-resistant, low-ghosting sheet-steel surface (painted or enamelled), a torsion-resistant anodised aluminium frame with plastic corners, a continuous pen tray and a tip-resistant base with lockable castors.
