Concrete power trowel, walk-behind, petrol engine, approx. 900 mm diameter

Walk-behind power trowel for smoothing and densifying freshly placed concrete floors and screeds.

The power trowel smooths and closes the surface of freshly placed concrete floors and industrial screeds with rotating blades and is indispensable for floor slabs, hall floors and yard areas. The machine must be delivered with CE marking, a declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive and the Outdoor Noise Directive, EU engine approval (Stage V) and a German operating manual.
