Rechargeable LED flashlight (USB-C)

Robust aluminium flashlight with Li-ion battery, several brightness levels and USB-C charging.

The rechargeable flashlight lights up shafts, basements, ceiling voids and plant rooms where site lighting does not reach. The lamp is not suitable for explosive atmospheres unless it carries an ATEX approval.

Beam distance150-250 m

Run timeapprox.; 2 h (turbo) to over 30 h (lowest level)
