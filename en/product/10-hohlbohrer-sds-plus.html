SDS-plus hollow drill bit with dust extraction, Ø 12 mm x 350 mm (carbide, for dust class M extractors); Ø 14/16 mm as variant

Hollow drill bit with SDS-plus shank that carries drilling dust through the shaft into a dust class M extractor.

The hollow drill bit removes drilling dust at the point of origin through the hollow shank into the connected extractor and keeps quartz dust from concrete and masonry out of the breathing air. It is used with an SDS-plus rotary hammer and a dust class M site vacuum, together with a suction connector. Professional users expect a carbide cutting head with a clean centring tip, a true-running shank to the SDS-plus standard and a continuous suction channel without constrictions.
