Two-post vehicle lift 4.5 t, heavy duty hydraulic

The 4.5 t variant is designed for the heavier vehicles in the yard, such as vans with flatbed or box body, large SUVs and light commercial vehicles, and allows servicing and repair without an external workshop. Professional users in Germany expect reinforced, corrosion-protected columns, a reliable locking latch on both sides, overload protection in the hydraulic circuit and a 400 V drive unit. Required are the CE declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC, a test report to EN 1493 and exact foundation data (concrete grade and thickness), because the higher load stresses the floor more. The lift may only be operated after inspection before first use and with recurring inspection by competent persons (BetrSichV, DGUV).
