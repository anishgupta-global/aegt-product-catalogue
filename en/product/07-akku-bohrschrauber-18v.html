Cordless drill driver 18 V, brushless (2-speed)

Brushless 18 V cordless drill driver with 2-speed gearbox for drilling and driving in wood, metal and drywall – the everyday hand tool on every site.

The cordless drill driver is the most-used power tool in fit-out, installation and drywall work and runs in multi-shift operation on German sites. Professional users expect a brushless motor, a robust two-speed metal gearbox, finely adjustable torque, a keyless chuck and batteries with cell monitoring against overload, deep discharge and overheating.
