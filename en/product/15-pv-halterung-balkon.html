Aluminium PV module mount for balcony, flat roof and garden (set, adjustable tilt)

Aluminium mounting stand for framed PV modules with adjustable tilt, can be ballasted without roof penetration.

The aluminium mounting stand carries framed PV modules on a flat roof, garage roof, in the garden or on the balcony parapet and sets the tilt angle for more yield. The construction in aluminium with small parts in aluminium or stainless steel stays corrosion-resistant and needs no roof penetration when ballasted. For the German market, installation instructions, ballast data and GPSR labelling also count.
