Construction robotics

Interior wall and ceiling spraying robot (putty + latex paint)

Automatically applies putty and latex paint to interior walls and ceilings up to 3.2 m working height.

In Germany a uniform surface to DIN 18363, a masking concept for windows, doors and floors and execution to EN 1953 for spraying equipment are required. CE marking incl. radio, EMC and battery conformity is mandatory, and an ATEX assessment as well for solvent-based coatings.
