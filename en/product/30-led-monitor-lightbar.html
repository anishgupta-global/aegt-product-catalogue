Monitor light bar with asymmetric optics and adjustable colour temperature, USB

Slim USB light bar for the monitor edge: asymmetric optics, full-spectrum LED (Ra > 95), five colour temperatures from 3,000 to 7,150 K.

The monitor light bar is fixed to the screen edge with a counterweight clamp and lights the work surface through asymmetric optics without reflecting in or dazzling the screen. Power comes via USB (5 V, at most 5 W); the delivery includes a USB cable and manual, no power adapter.
