Orange-peel grapple with rotator for 20–24 t excavators

Five-tine, endlessly rotating orange-peel grapple (0.5 m³) for scrap, wood, stone and demolition debris.

The orange-peel grapple is the classic handling tool for scrap, wood, stone, slag and loose demolition material: several shells closing against each other enclose irregular loads so that nothing falls through. They also expect a CE-compliant attachment with a declaration of conformity, a mounting that matches the excavator (quick coupler or pin pattern), cleanly routed hydraulic lines and EN 10204 3.1 material certificates. Flow (60–80 l/min) and pressure (180–220 bar) must be matched to the data of the carrier excavator.
