Crawler excavator approx. 20 t (diesel)

20 t class crawler excavator for building pits, pipe trenches and bulk excavation in civil engineering.

The 20 t class crawler excavator is the standard machine for building pits, sewer and pipe trenches and bulk excavation in civil engineering. A premium unit offers an EU Stage V diesel engine, a stable tracked undercarriage, a hydraulic quick-coupler system with auxiliary hydraulic circuits for hammer and grab, and a comfortable cab with automatic climate control and rear and side cameras. Professional users in Germany expect full CE marking with a declaration of conformity, ROPS/FOPS-tested operator protection, documented sound power levels and a German-language manual with a secured spare-parts supply.
