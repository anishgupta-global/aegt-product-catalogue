Smart pet feeder with app/WLAN (3–6 l, optional camera)

Wi-Fi feeder for cat and dog with app schedule, portion control and optional HD camera with two-way audio.

The smart feeder portions dry food on a schedule and is controlled via app over Wi-Fi; the camera version adds a wide-angle camera overlooking the bowl, optionally with microphone and speaker for two-way audio. High-quality units have a stainless-steel bowl, a removable washable hopper, jam detection and a battery backup for power cuts. Premium buyers expect a mature app with German-language interface, GDPR-compliant cloud connection and updates. Legally it is radio equipment (RED) with a power supply and, with a camera, data-protection relevant.
