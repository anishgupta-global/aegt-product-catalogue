Ceiling drilling robot (BIM-driven, with dust extraction)

Drills anchor and dowel holes in ceilings and walls under BIM control with integrated dust extraction.

The ceiling drilling robot drills holes for dowels and anchors in ceilings and walls under BIM control, precisely and with integrated dust extraction – the overhead work that strains crews the most. Professional users expect millimetre-range positioning by total station, class M dust extraction with attention to TRGS 559 (quartz dust) and suitability for drilling holes to ETA anchor specifications. CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, a safety controller to EN ISO 13849-1 and a German manual are mandatory.
