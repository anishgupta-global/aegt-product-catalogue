Polyurethane protective mat for plate compactors, bolt-on

Bolt-on protective mat of cast polyurethane that protects paving and slabs from scratches and marks during compaction.

The protective mat of cast polyurethane is bolted under the base plate of the plate compactor and protects paving stones, concrete slabs and natural stone from scratches, scuff marks and chipped corners during compaction.

Technical data

MaterialCast polyurethane, not rubber
HardnessShore A per ISO 868
Dimensions and hole pattern14 kg; 500 x 350 mm
FixingBolt-on
SurfaceNon-marking on light paving and slabs
ApplicationCompacting concrete block paving, slabs and natural stone; upgrade to the rubber mat
