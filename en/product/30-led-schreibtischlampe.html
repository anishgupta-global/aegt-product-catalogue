LED desk lamp, dimmable, colour temperature 2700–6000 K, touch control, foldable, clip-on

Foldable clip-on LED lamp with touch dimming and three colour temperatures from 2700 to 6000 K for home office, study and bedside.

Compact, foldable desk lamp with a clamp base for table tops and shelf edges; brightness is controlled by a touch sensor and the colour temperature is chosen from three steps (2700 / 4000 / 6000 K). With a maximum of 5 W it works as supplementary task lighting for a height-adjustable desk. For EU distribution CE (LVD/EMC), RoHS, an EU responsible person under GPSR and, if a battery is fitted, the Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 must be observed.
