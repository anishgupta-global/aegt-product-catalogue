6-bay desktop multi-charger for handheld two-way radios (Li-ion)

The pockets are individually replaceable and the power supply (5 V / 4 A) has an exchangeable plug for international use. For construction sites, events, hotels and warehouses it is the standard complement to the radio set because it saves charging cables, mix-ups and single power supplies. Professional buyers look for an exact pocket fit for the radio series, a CE-compliant power supply with ecodesign evidence and secured spare-part supply for the bays.
