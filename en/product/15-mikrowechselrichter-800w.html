Microinverter 800 VA for balcony power plants (Wi-Fi, 2 MPPT, Schuko connection cable)

Compact 800 VA microinverter (IP67, 2 MPPT, Wi-Fi) for two solar modules, the mandatory part of every plug-in balcony power plant.

The microinverter converts the DC of up to two solar modules directly at the module into grid-compliant 230 V AC and limits feed-in to the 800 VA permitted for plug-in solar devices.
