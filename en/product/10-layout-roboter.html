Layout and scribing robot (total-station or RTK guided)

Transfers CAD/BIM plans true to scale onto the floor and automatically marks walls, axes and installation points.

The layout robot transfers CAD and BIM plans true to scale onto the floor slab: wall axes, door openings, installation and fit-out positions are positioned by total station or RTK-GNSS and marked directly with ink or paint. It replaces chalk lines and manual setting-out and saves surveyor and foreman time on large floors. Electrics, radio (RTK / remote control) and laser must be documented as CE-compliant.
