Paper towel dispenser, wall-mounted, ABS, lockable

Lockable wall dispenser for folded paper towels (Z/C-fold) with inspection window for staff and sanitary rooms on construction sites and in workshops.

The paper towel dispenser in impact-resistant ABS supplies washrooms on construction sites, in welfare containers and workshops with folded paper towels in a hygienic way. The lockable housing with inspection window protects against vandalism and shows the fill level at a glance. A professional user in Germany expects a sturdy wall mounting, handling of common Z/C-fold towels, material compliance under REACH and clean product labelling under the GPSR.
