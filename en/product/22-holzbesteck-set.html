Disposable wooden cutlery (birch): fork, knife, spoon, 160 mm

Smooth-sanded disposable birch cutlery as a permitted alternative to plastic cutlery.

Good cutlery is sanded splinter-free, tastes neutral, does not break when cutting soft food and is packed dry and mould-free. Professional buyers in Germany expect FSC- or PEFC-certified wood with traceable origin and evidence of food contact compliance under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004. Coatings or wax are only permitted if they are not plastic within the meaning of the Single-Use Plastics Directive.
