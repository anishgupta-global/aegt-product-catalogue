Manual hand pallet truck 2.5 t

Robust manual pallet truck for Euro pallets that lifts loads of up to 2.5 t hydraulically and is moved by hand.

The hand pallet truck is basic equipment on every construction site and in every materials store: it moves Euro pallets carrying building materials, tool boxes or waste containers without power and without a driving licence. Professional units have a welded steel-profile frame, a sealed hydraulic pump with overload valve, polyurethane wheels and fork-tip rollers for entering pallets. A regular inspection by a competent person under the BetrSichV is recommended.
